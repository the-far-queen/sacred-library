## Honest statement of the relationship

far-law's 13 directories are **not** Yale's 12 areas. The repo adds six
(Intellectual Property, Legislation & Policy, Litigation, Tax, Labor, and a
Commercial split from Corporate) and omits four (Human Rights, Law &
Economics, Law Teaching, Legal History).

Intellectual Property, Legislation & Policy, Litigation, and Tax — the four
divisions in the original brief — are **not** named Yale areas. They are
defensible as an editorial taxonomy, but claiming they are "Yale Law School
divisions" would be inaccurate.

**Legal position:** subject-area names are not copyrightable, and no Yale
material is reproduced here. This is an organizational choice and implies no
endorsement by Yale. This file exists so the divergence is explicit rather
than implied.
