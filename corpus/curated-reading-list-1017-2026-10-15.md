## 1. THE METHOD, IN BOBBY'S WORDS

From `teachings.php`, verbatim:

> "Research materials, locate sources, weight value, choose teachers" /
> "Read literature" / "Study news online, browse video and article content" /
> "Reading biographies and hagiographies" /
> "Meeting learned monks, and asking for instruction or sitting nearby" /
> "Studying sacred texts" / "Study Dhamma, read texts by achievers"

And the operative sentence:

> **"You can navigate towards the center through reading great authors, who may
> reference other great authors, doing a lot of the finding and discrimination
> for us."**

**Authors are pointers to other authors. The skill being trained is
discrimination, not accumulation.** That single line is why this list matters:
a list of 1,017 is not a deliverable, and anyone who treats it as one has
misread it.

The unit of progress is stated in the same file:

> "One becomes proficient only in that which one has studied. Study
> hagiographies and biographies, not just the account of their lives, although
> that as well, **but the description of their realization, constituting a
> narrative and a teaching.**"

Bobby also ships with his own failure modes pre-written, which is why the
library is not naive:

> "Sacred texts acting as guidebooks have been written and maintained, passed on
> successfully through well-guarded lineages. **In some cases, having been
> misinterpreted, reworked or ritualized by those without direct
> understanding.**"
> "It's one thing to have valid texts, but another thing for materially-minded
> people to study and take to heart lessons that may not seem applicable to
> their pursuits."

  is a precise statement of what the far-* schools are for.

## 5. WHAT IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT, AND WHY IT MATTERS

- **The commercial cluster repeats** across two separate blocks — *Law of
  Attraction*, *Think and Grow Rich*, *The Secret*, Esther Hicks twice. **List
  density is itself a signal.** Treat repetition as a warning, not a
  recommendation.
- **New Age texts are listed densely but held thinly.** Two or three physical
  volumes on the shelf for ~40 listed entries.
- **The 19th-century literary void.** In 1,017 entries, **Dickens appears once**
  (*Oliver Twist*) and **Borges not at all.** I independently chose Dickens
  tonight — correctly, and not from this list. **I was working from the shelf,
  not the syllabus.** That gap is worth naming rather than filling silently.

## 6. HOW THIS IS USED

**Not as a booklist.** The list is a set of pointers; the work is what
`teachings.php` describes — find, discriminate, and *assimilate deeply into
one's own constitution*. Per the qualification protocol in
`far-site/qualifications/`, each text used must produce:

1. the claim, in the source's own words
2. the axis it moves
3. the delta, quoted and anchored
4. the failure mode it argues against
5. the test that would show it took

**Four of five is a note.** That test came from the failure of the session that
produced this file.
